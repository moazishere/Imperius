Questions

Before you ask.

Do we need to change our systems?

No. Imperius connects to what you already use, whether that's a POS, an accounting system or Excel sheets. Your team keeps working the same way.

How is this different from a chatbot?

A chatbot reads your files and guesses. Imperius first builds clean, verified figures from your records, applies your business rules, and only then uses AI to explain the result. Every answer traces back to its source.

Can we ask questions in Arabic?

Yes. Ask in Arabic or English and get the answer in the same language.

What if our data is messy?

Most data is. Cleaning and joining scattered records is the first thing Imperius does, and it's part of every pilot.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on how many data sources and branches you connect. We agree on it with you after the pilot, once you've seen the value.
